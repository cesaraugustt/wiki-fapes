## (2) INTRODUCTION

The European Partnership "FOrests, forestry, and the forest-based sector: REsearch and innovation for a Sustainable Transition (FOREST)" aims to mobilise research and innovation to accelerate the transition to a sustainable forest bioeconomy. Serving as the primary instrument for collaboration among stakeholders in EU Member States and Associated Countries; the partnership will facilitate coordinated research and innovation (R&amp;l) actions in order to reduce fragmentation and foster innovation and competitiveness in the forest-based sector , supporting climate-smart and sustainable forest management and its transformation from a resource-intensive to a knowledge-intensive; productive, resource-efficient; and resilient sector.

The partnership is strategically aligned with European policy frameworks; including the European Green Deal, the EU Forest Strategy for 2030, the EU Biodiversity Strategy for 2030, and the EU Bioeconomy Strategy. positioned research to advance EU policy developments in climate resilience; biodiversity , forest   monitoring, and the circular economy. Building on the   overarching aim; the partnership's ambition is to launch and coordinate seven co-funded Joint Calls over a ten-year period (2026-2036) funding transnational projects that align closely with key EU strategic priorities to support a sustainable transition of forests, forestry and the forest-based sector. Guided by a Strategic Research and Innovation Agenda (SRIA') developed through broad stakeholder engagement; this initiative will ensure that R&amp;l interventions are centrally coordinated and strategically impactful.  The Joint Calls will lead to the funding of research projects while facilitating transnational collaboration between a variety of stakeholders . key

Research Funding Organisations (RFOs) from 27 countries.

The participating RFOs have provisionally reserved approximately €33 million for this call. Together with the EU co-contribution; this provides for a total indicative Joint Call budget of up to €40 million.

The scope and topics of the Joint Call derive from the official Strategic Research and Innovation Agenda of the partnership and were selected by the Research Funding Organisations (RFOs) participating in the Joint Call.

This first Joint Call is intentionally broad. This allows the flexibility to accommodate different research and innovation needs while fostering synergies between these areas and aligning with SRIA goals. The following topics are included and described in detail in Chapter (4) Topics.

- TOPIC 1 Climate-Resilient Forest Systems
- TOPIC 2 Adaptive and Multifunctional Forest Management Systems
- TOPIC 3 Timber and Innovative Wood-Based Products
- TOPIC 4 Forest Value Chains, Markets and Governance

FOREST will take all lawful steps to ensure confidentiality of information and documents obtained during the evaluation and selection procedures of the Joint Calls. Personal data collected within the Joint Calls are properly managed to comply with EU regulations .

## (4) IOPICS EXPECTED IMPACTS OF THE CALL

## Call Objectives

Forests play a critical role in climate   regulation; biodiversity   conservation;   ecosystem services; and the disturbances , and competing societal demands. Addressing these challenges requires coordinated and policy-relevant research and innovation that strengthens resilience; sustainability, and the multifunctional role of forests.

The call is aligned with European policy frameworks; including the European Green Deal, the EU Forest Strategy for 2030, the EU Biodiversity Strategy for 2030, and the EU Bioeconomy Strategy; as well as related policy developments relevant to climate; biodiversity , forest monitoring; and the circular economy. key

This joint call supports transnational research and innovation activities across the spectrum from fundamental research to applied research and, where appropriate; demonstration activities. Projects are expected to generate new knowledge; develop practical solutions; and contribute to implementation in policy, management; or industry contexts, while considering diversity of forests and forest-based sector .

Proposals should clearly demonstrate their relevance to one or more of the call topics and associated objectives and, where relevant; explain how the proposed activities contribute to broader European policy priorities and practical implementation needs. Projects are also encouraged, where appropriate; to contribute to the sciencepolicy interface through tools; methodologies; indicators; or other relevant outputs .

## IL. Topic areas

Applicants must select the single topic that best reflects the primary focus of their proposal.

The selected topic determines the evaluation panel and corresponding ranking list. Proposals may address multiple topics but will be evaluated and ranked under one primary topic. Evaluation criteria will be applied consistently across all topics.

The topics provide strategic direction and facilitate evaluation:  They are not exhaustive and do not restrict the submission of innovative or cross-cutting proposals aligned with the overall objectives of the call.

## IV. ACCESS TO GENETIC RESOURCES AND BENEFIT-SHARING
