## FOREST Joint Call 2026 : FOREST Pathways

## First FOREST Partnership Joint Call launches with up to €40 million for transnational forest research and innovation

FORESTPathways brings together 36 research funding organisations from 27 countries to support collaborative research and innovation for resilient forests and sustainable forest-based futures

## 15 September 2026

<!-- page 2 -->

"

The European Partnership FOrests, forestry, and the forest-based sector: REsearch

and innovation for a Sustainable Transition (FOREST) has today launched its first Joint Call for transnational research and innovation projects. Entitled FORESTPathways - Building Knowledge and Solutions for Resilient Forests and Sustainable Forest-Based Futures, the call brings together 36 Research Funding Organisations from 27 countries, with an indicative total public funding commitment of up to €40 million, including the European Union co-contribution. The call marks an important milestone for the FOREST Partnership, which commenced on 1 June 2026 and brings together organisations from across Europe and beyond to coordinate research and innovation for forests, forestry and the forest-based sector. By bringing together funding organisations and research communities across countries and connecting research communities across disciplines and sectors, this first Joint Call creates a unique opportunity to generate the knowledge, innovations and collaborations needed for resilient forests and a sustainable and a sustainable and competitive forest-based sector.
