Biodiversa+ is pleased to announce that its 6th annual Joint Call for transnational research projects BiodivFuture is now open. Co-funded by the European Commission, this call, alongside a range of actions under the Biodiversa+ Flagship Programmes, will contribute to achieving one of Biodiversa+'s strategic objectives: producing actionable knowledge to drive transformative change and halt and reverse biodiversity decline.

The online submission platform (EPSS) for pre-proposals is now open.
